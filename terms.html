Academy Regulations

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Standards of membership, fair play code, tournament participation, and tuition agreements at CHECKMATE.

Updated: January 2026

By enrolling in CHECKMATE Chess Academy programs, accessing our digital learning portals, or competing in our organized tournaments, you agree to comply with the terms and standards detailed below.

1. Academy Membership & Cohort Enrollment

Admission into specific cohorts (Junior Foundation, Intermediate Club, or Elite Masterclass) is determined by faculty assessment. We reserve the right to recommend cohort reassignments based on a student's Elo progression, commitment, and diagnostic evaluations.

2. Code of Sportsmanship & Fair Play

Chess is a game of intellectual honor. All members, parents, and coaches must strictly adhere to the FIDE Code of Ethics:

  • Zero Tolerance for Engine Assistance: Use of external engines (Stockfish, Komodo, etc.) or assistance during rated sparring or tournament rounds results in immediate expulsion.
  • Respectful Conduct: Unsportsmanlike conduct, verbal abuse, intentional distraction of opponents, or failure to shake hands before/after rated matches will not be tolerated.
  • Parental Boundaries: Spectating guardians must remain outside designated tournament competition areas and avoid interfering with arbiter decisions.

3. Tuition Fees & Cancellation Policy

Monthly and annual tuition fees are billed in advance. All paid plans include access to study material, live classrooms, and periodic tournaments:

  • 14-Day Satisfaction Guarantee: New scholars can request a 100% full tuition refund within 14 days of their first paid session if unsatisfied.
  • Pause Option: Scholars taking exam leaves or traveling for national championships may freeze their enrollment for up to 30 days per calendar year.
  • Notice Period: Cancellation of recurring subscriptions requires written notice at least 7 days before the next billing cycle.

4. Intellectual Property & Curricular Materials

All masterclass recordings, opening novelty files, annotated PGN databases, and tactical exercises provided to students remain the exclusive intellectual property of CHECKMATE Chess Academy. Unauthorized public redistribution or commercial resale is strictly prohibited.

5. Tournament Participation & FIDE Regulations

All sanctioned tournaments hosted by CHECKMATE are governed by official FIDE Laws of Chess. The Chief Arbiter's rulings on touch-move rules, clock disputes, and claims are final.

6. Contact & Legal Disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of India and international arbitration standards. Any questions regarding enrollment policies should be directed to legal@checkmate.academy.
