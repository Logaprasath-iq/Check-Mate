Legal & Compliance

PRIVACY POLICY

How CHECKMATE Chess Academy protects your personal information, competitive telemetry, and student privacy.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At CHECKMATE Chess Academy, we take your trust seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of data we collect, how we safeguard your information, and your rights as a student, parent, or competitive scholar.

1. Information We Collect

When you enroll in our programs, reserve a diagnostic evaluation, or utilize our online digital platform, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identity: Full student name, guardian name (for minors under 18), email address, and verified phone number.
  • Chess Rating & Competitive Profile: FIDE ID number, national federation rating, online handle, and tournament performance history.
  • Game Telemetry & Training Data: PGN notation, blunder telemetry from AI engines, puzzle solve times, and curriculum completion metrics.
  • Billing Information: Transaction identifiers and payment status processed securely through encrypted PCI-compliant gateways.

2. Protection of Minors (COPPA & Child Safety)

Over 70% of our junior academy scholars are under the age of 18. We strictly adhere to international child online privacy guidelines. Parental or legal guardian consent is mandatory for all accounts registered for students under 16 years of age. We never publicly display contact details, addresses, or private communications of minor students.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected information solely for pedagogical and competitive purposes:

  • Conducting personalized tactical coaching and game reviews with Grandmaster faculty.
  • Pairing tournament rounds and submitting official Elo rating reports to FIDE and national chess federations.
  • Providing customized puzzle difficulty curves based on neural network error patterns.
  • Sending critical schedule updates, tournament schedules, and diagnostic feedback reports.

4. Strict No-Sale & Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, or monetize student personal data to third-party advertisers or external data brokers under any circumstances. Data is only shared with authorized tournament arbitration bodies when officially registering scholars for FIDE-rated championships.

5. Data Security & Storage

All student records and game databases are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and transmitted using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Access is restricted to authorized faculty coaches and staff under strict non-disclosure obligations.

6. Your Rights & Data Inquiries

You retain full rights to inspect, update, export, or request deletion of your student account records at any time. For privacy inquiries or parental access requests, contact our Compliance Officer at privacy@checkmate.academy.
