Checkmate Autumn Invitational: Game Analysis and Round 7 Deciders

FM Arjun Mehta
Chief Arbiter & Academy Tournament Director

The 2026 Checkmate Autumn Invitational concluded on Sunday with nail-biting finishes across all top tables. With ₹500,000 in total prizes and crucial FIDE rating points on the line, the 7-round Swiss event brought together 64 titled and aspiring masters from seven countries.

Going into the final round, International Master hopeful Vikramaditya Sen (FIDE 2420) and WGM Elena Rostova (FIDE 2395) were tied for first place at 5.5/6. Board 1 was set for a championship battle that will be studied in academy seminars for years to come.

Board 1: The Move 34 Shockwave

Opting for the Semi-Slav Meran variation, Rostova constructed a rock-solid central blockade. But Sen, playing White, methodically gathered piece power toward the kingside with h4-h5 and a rook lift to g3. Under severe time trouble on move 34 with only 48 seconds remaining on the clock, Sen unleashed an astonishing thematic thunderbolt: 34.Qxh7+!!

The queen sacrifice forcibly stripped the black monarch of pawn cover, allowing White's rooks and bishop to construct an unstoppable mating net along the open h-file and 7th rank.

Tactical Diagram: Sen's Decisive 34.Qxh7+! Queen Sacrifice Move 34.Qxh7+!

Position after 34.Qxh7+! Black is forced to capture 34...Kxh7 35.Rh3+ Kg8 36.Rh8# ending the championship contest.

Final Podium Standings

  • 1st Place (Champion): Vikramaditya Sen — 6.5 / 7 (₹200,000 + Trophy)
  • 2nd Place: Elena Rostova — 5.5 / 7 (₹125,000)
  • 3rd Place: GM Marcus Chen — 5.5 / 7 (₹75,000 on tiebreak)
  • Top Junior (U18): Aryan Joshi (Checkmate Academy) — 5.0 / 7 (+42 FIDE Elo points)
