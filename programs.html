Grandmaster Pedagogy

TRAINING PROGRAMS

From foundational piece mechanics to FIDE title preparation: find your customized pathway to mastery.

BEGINNER FOUNDATION

"Master piece geometry, board control, and essential mates."

  • Rating Target: 0 – 1200 Elo
  • Duration: 3 Months (24 Classes)
  • Frequency: 2 Sessions / Week (60 min)
  • Lead Coach: Sofia Williams
  • Tuition: ₹999 / mo
ENROLL IN BEGINNER

Syllabus Modules:

Module 1: Rules, Board Vision & Tactical Safety
Board coordinate mastery, value of pieces, pawn promotion, castling, en passant, detecting undefended pieces, avoiding 1-move blunders.
Module 2: Fundamental Checkmating Patterns
Ladder mate (2 Rooks), King and Queen vs lone King, King and Rook box method, back-rank mates, scholar's mate defense.
Module 3: The Golden Opening Principles
Control the center, develop knights before bishops, king safety before attacking, avoiding moving the same piece twice in the opening.

INTERMEDIATE TACTICS

"Sharpen dynamic calculation and recognize combinations instantly."

  • Rating Target: 1200 – 1600 Elo
  • Duration: 6 Months (48 Classes)
  • Frequency: 3 Sessions / Week (75 min)
  • Lead Coach: Daniel Carter
  • Tuition: ₹1,499 / mo
ENROLL IN INTERMEDIATE

Syllabus Modules:

Module 1: The Tactical Arsenal
Pins (absolute vs relative), knight forks, skewers, discovery checks, double attacks, deflection, overloading defenders.
Module 2: Building a Reliable Opening Repertoire
1.e4 options (Italian Game, Scotch, Caro-Kann fundamentals), 1.d4 setups (Queen's Gambit Declined, London System counterplay).
Module 3: Essential Pawn Structures
Isolated queen pawn (IQP) play, passed pawns, backward pawns, doubled pawns, minority attacks, pawn breaks.
Flagship Program

ADVANCED STRATEGY

"Master positional prophylaxis, complex endgames, and tournament psychology."

  • Rating Target: 1600 – 2000 Elo
  • Duration: 9 Months (72 Classes)
  • Frequency: 4 Sessions / Week (90 min)
  • Lead Coach: Rahul Sharma
  • Tuition: ₹1,999 / mo
ENROLL IN ADVANCED

Syllabus Modules:

Module 1: Prophylactic Thinking (Nimzowitsch & Petrosian)
Identifying opponent threats 3 moves ahead, overprotection of key outposts, piece sacrifices for positional grip, blockade techniques.
Module 2: Complete Theoretical Opening Preparation
Sicilian Defense (Najdorf, Dragon), King's Indian Defense, French Defense Winawer, modern engine novelties up to move 20.
Module 3: Complex Minor Piece & Rook Endgames
Lucena bridge, Philidor 3rd rank defense, Vancura defense, good vs bad bishops, bishop vs knight dynamic balances.

ELITE MASTERCLASS

"Train like a tournament professional aiming for FIDE Master and IM titles."

  • Rating Target: 2000+ FIDE Elo
  • Duration: 12 Months (Comprehensive)
  • Frequency: 5 Sessions / Week + 1-on-1 GM Mentorship
  • Lead Coach: Arjun Mehta (FM)
  • Tuition: ₹3,999 / mo
APPLY FOR ELITE

Syllabus Modules:

Module 1: Engine-Assisted Repertoire Construction
Depth 40+ cloud evaluations, customized novelty traps, surprise weapons against specific opponents, psychological repertoire design.
Module 2: Classical Grandmaster Heritage Study
In-depth study of Botvinnik, Smyslov, Fischer, Karpov, and Carlsen's converting methodologies and positional squeezes.
Module 3: Peak Tournament Psychology & Stamina
Time management crisis control, handling double-round exhaustion, recovering from painful losses, Swiss tournament point optimization.
